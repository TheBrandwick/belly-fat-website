About BellyFat

BellyFat is a mobile app for people who have tried calorie counting before and quit. Instead of dumping a food database on you and wishing you luck, it starts with a short set of questions about your body, your routine, your sleep and stress, the foods you avoid and what has gone wrong for you in the past — then builds a plan around those answers.

The focus is abdominal fat specifically, because that's the fat most closely tied to how you feel day to day. So the app tracks waist alongside weight, keeps an eye on late-night hunger and meal timing, and scores your day on habits rather than on a single calorie number.

It's built by The Brandwick and available on iOS and Android. We're a small team, we read every message, and the roadmap is shaped by what users ask for.
