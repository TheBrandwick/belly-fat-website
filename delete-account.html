Delete your account

App: BellyFat  ·  Developer: The Brandwick  ·  Last updated: 5 September 2026

In short. You can delete your BellyFat account and everything in it at any time — from inside the app in about 30 seconds, or by emailing privacy@thebrandwick.com if you can no longer open the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Before you start: cancel your subscription

If you have an active BellyFat subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so they must be cancelled where you bought them — otherwise you will keep being charged after your account is gone.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → BellyFat → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BellyFat → Cancel.

Cancel first, then come back and delete your account.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the BellyFat app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete.
  5. If you have been signed in for a while, you will be asked to confirm it is you — enter your password, or re-approve with Apple or Google. This is a security step required before an account can be removed.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are returned to the welcome screen. Nothing further is needed from you.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Use this if you have lost access to the app, changed phones, or cannot sign in.

  1. Email privacy@thebrandwick.com with the subject line "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address you used to sign up, if you still can — that is the quickest way for us to confirm it is your account.
  3. Tell us how you signed in: email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email, include the relay address (it ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com).

We may ask one or two questions to verify the request before we act on it — we will never delete an account on an unverified request. We aim to respond within 30 days, and usually far sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile — name, email, avatar, and the answers you gave during onboarding
  • Your body measurements, goals and weight history
  • Your food logs and meal history
  • Your daily scores, daily tasks and streaks
  • Your preferences, including avoided ingredients and reminder settings
  • All data cached on your device

Can I get my account back?

No. Deletion is permanent and there is no undo or recovery window. If you install BellyFat again later you will start fresh, with a new account and no previous history — even if you sign up with the same email address.
